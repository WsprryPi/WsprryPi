# Wsprry Pi 4.0 Beta 1

**Candidate documentation — not yet published.** This draft describes the
candidate being prepared from `devel`, compared with published
[v3.2.0](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/releases/tag/v3.2.0).
Candidate acceptance and download publication are separate gates. The
[evidence ledger](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/release_tools/4.0-Beta-1-Release-Evidence.md) records exact identities,
validation, issue dispositions and outstanding checks.

## What is new

- **Raspberry Pi 5 RP1 GPCLK support.** WsprryPi consumes the independently
  released [RP1-GPCLK-DKMS 1.0.0](https://github.com/WsprryPi/RP1-GPCLK-DKMS/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
  provider, with GPIO4/GPIO20 route administration, ownership-aware installation
  and removal, restart restoration and supplemental qualification reporting.
  Installation and route selection do not enable RF output. Provider compatibility,
  application eligibility and recorded RF evidence are separate states. [#412](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/412)
- **Pico connections and Fleet.** Discover and retain device profiles, select a
  USB, Plain LAN or authenticated network connection, and manage the selected
  Pico explicitly. Management includes station and hardware resources, Type 1/2/3
  WSPR planning on supporting firmware, consumer login/recovery, USB name and
  Wi-Fi management, qualified USB UTC delivery, and GPIO drive settings.
  Saving a device profile does not connect it or enable transmission. The Pi
  WTP endpoint can also coordinate up to eight independent output assignments
  alongside its local schedule. [Pico integration](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/wtp-production-integration.md),
  [Fleet](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/wtp-fleet.md), [Pi endpoint](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/wtp-pi-operation.md)
- **Independent Pico station drafts.** On supported firmware, Fleet can save
  valid callsign, locator and reported-power fields while other fields remain
  incomplete or invalid. Per-field feedback preserves rejected and newer edits.
  **Apply saved station progress** is a separate action using device-saved
  values. Consumer profile-owned station application remains in Pico setup;
  older firmware retains the complete-save workflow. Saving drafts does not
  activate output, resume STOP or establish transmit readiness.
- **Selected Pico firmware over USB.** Check a published image before an explicit
  install, follow a retained BOOTSEL migration when needed, and recover a retained
  transaction. Firmware maintenance blocks conflicting application mutations.
  Explicit full-flash erase verifies blank flash and leaves the Pico in BOOTSEL;
  it removes firmware and persistent data. Compatible installer updates are
  independent of Pi releases, while active recovery retains its original verified
  installer. The firmware selector defaults to Pico **main**, independently of
  the Pi branch; unavailable images are refused without substitution.
  [USB installation and recovery](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/pico-usb-installer.md)
- **Canonical bands and WSPR preferences.** One 19-band catalog, from 2200 m
  through 70 cm, drives correlation and policy. Default, named-preset and custom
  dial-frequency preferences retain explicit provenance; 60 m has legacy and
  WRC-15 choices. 8 m and 5 m require deliberate preferences. Correlation and
  software eligibility do not determine regional operating permission. [#332](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/332)
- **Private support workflow.** Create, review and download a local support
  bundle, including point-in-time process/resource evidence. Explicit consent
  can encrypt the exact reviewed bundle for private intake using a signed
  destination policy. Local collection remains useful if private intake is
  unavailable; creation does not automatically upload a bundle. [#352](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/352),
  [#359](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/359), [#414](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/414)
- **Hardware-free development.** Explicit `--backend simulated` exercises the
  real application planning and lifecycle paths without transmitter hardware.
  Backend build profiles, component tests and Linux CI cover software contracts;
  simulation is never an automatic fallback for failed or absent hardware. [#400](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/400)

## Changes and fixes

- Test Tone supports a band or exact custom RF frequency and preserves the
  inactive mode. Scheduler ordering, watchdog cleanup and result/restoration
  feedback distinguish requested work from confirmed outcomes. Legacy GPIO TONE
  no longer emits the lowest WSPR-table tone 2.197265625 Hz below the requested
  RF frequency; synthesis quantization and calibration still apply. Si5351 planner
  errors are contained rather than terminating the service. [#361](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/361),
  [#368](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/368), [#446](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/446)
- GPIO correction separates the system-clock frequency estimate, its residual,
  manual fallback and intrinsic chipset/parent-clock defaults. Positive GPIO PPM
  describes a fast source clock and corrects requested RF downward. Corrections
  remain frozen for each transmission. Si5351 uses its own reference and manual
  correction; it does not consume the GPIO system-clock estimate. [#387](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/387),
  [#388](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/388), [#429](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/429)
- Si5351 supports explicit reference-source/crystal-load configuration, detected
  bus/address selection and readiness checks. GPIO26/27 are available to the
  safe-pin selectors; GPIO4/20 reservation follows actual RF ownership, and
  compatible band assignments can share a GPIO. Startup quiescence attempts
  to disable transmitter output before ordinary operation. [#382](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/382),
  [#346](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/346), [#347](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/347),
  [#348](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/348), [#365](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/365)
- Completed WebSocket clients are reaped, and log-stream cleanup ends owned
  `journalctl` children after disconnects. Setup save feedback tracks newer
  pending edits, frequency controls work on narrow screens, and Spots offers
  WSPR and QRSS choices. Update detection requires release or ancestry evidence;
  a different SHA alone does not prove an upgrade. [#349](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/349),
  [#355](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/355), [#343](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/343),
  [#367](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/367), [#354](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/354),
  [#364](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/364)
- Pico configuration saves negotiate supported preservation/update contracts.
  Older peers retain understood complete-save behavior; unsupported extensions
  refuse safely. Stale or lost replies preserve drafts and require explicit
  readback before another change. [#447](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/447),
  [#448](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/448)
- UI identity is content-addressed independently of the installed executable
  and running service. Upgrades retain evidence of local UI modifications and
  refresh feedback avoids stale-service refresh loops. [#422](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/422)
- Low-memory source installs perform resource preflight and can allocate
  temporary build swap automatically. Precompiled installs omit application
  compilation and its swap work. Installation repairs supported persistent or
  runtime service masks and refuses unsafe recovery states. Unreadable Pi
  revision data no longer fabricates a BCM2835 mailbox identity. [#428](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/428),
  [#434](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/434), [#430](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/430)
- The WSPR reference encoder clears stale callsign bytes when an instance is
  reused. Firmware download feedback identifies GitHub rate limits and retry
  times without automatically retrying a write or download.

## Upgrade and compatibility guidance

1. **Back up before upgrading.** Retain your current checkout/release, executable,
   INI, local UI customizations, relevant provider/boot state and private Pico
   recovery material. Stop scheduling output and resolve any retained firmware
   maintenance transaction before changing packages. The default installer still
   follows the stable branch; it does not select this unpublished candidate.
2. **Review calibration after migration.** Persistence migrates legacy
   `GPIO.Use NTP` to provider-neutral GPIO estimate settings. Scripts should use
   `--use-system-clock-frequency-estimate` instead of removed `--use-ntp`.
   GPIO estimate residual, manual fallback and Si5351 correction are separate.
   A previously measured total manual correction must account for the intrinsic
   chipset term to avoid applying it twice. Time synchronization or PPS alone
   does not measure or calibrate RF frequency.
3. **Review network access and inbound control.** Privileged browser operations
   use the Apache proxy and directly connected LAN policy. Off-LAN/reverse-proxy
   arrangements can be refused; the UI does not fall back to direct backend
   ports. This is network-location control, not individual authentication or
   encryption. The managed Plain LAN WTP listener defaults on after confirmed
   startup quiescence, on port 31417 with automatic physical-interface selection.
   Review `[WTP Server]` and disable it if inbound LAN control is unwanted. Local
   transmission ownership and output-safety gates still apply. [#416](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/416),
   [#426](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/426)
4. **Select the actual platform and dependencies.** Precompiled targets are
   ARMv6 hard-float and AArch64, each on Bookworm and Trixie userspace. Bullseye
   and separate ARMv7 exports are outside this matrix. Avahi libraries support
   discovery. Pico firmware maintenance requires Python 3.10+ and USB-enabled
   picotool 2.3 or newer within 2.x; Pi deployment does not install picotool or a
   Pico SDK. RP1 module installation additionally needs compatible kernel headers
   and DKMS/build tools. [Container builds](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/container-builds.md),
   [precompiled installation](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/blob/devel/docs/precompiled-installation.md)
5. **Use one ordinary clone.** First-party UI and reusable libraries are now
   ordinary parent-repository components. Recursive submodule initialization or
   independent component pulls are no longer the update procedure. Component
   paths, interfaces, standalone tests and third-party notices remain. [#415](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/415)

The retired 22 m band entry is removed; `lf` and `mf` remain aliases for 2200 m
and 630 m. Standard Feld-Hell, Linux stable CP2112 adapter identity completion,
native macOS CP2112 transport, direct PPS RF calibration and an older-BCM stock
kernel DKMS replacement are not added by this Beta. No blanket removal of the
existing WSPR/QRSS/FSKCW/DFCW workflows is intended.

## Rollback and qualification limits

Reinstalling a retained older executable is not a full rollback of packages,
configuration migrations, provider/boot files, UI content or Pico flash state.
Restore compatible supporting files and your pre-upgrade backups as a reviewed
set. Do not downgrade while a Pico firmware transaction is unresolved or delete
its maintenance marker to bypass recovery. Pico saved-progress formats and
credential drafts may survive a firmware downgrade; consult the firmware's
reset/downgrade guidance before relying on older firmware to read or erase them.

Band/mode eligibility follows the maintained backend qualification table.
Si5351's accepted 2 m/6 m/4 m evidence does not qualify GPIO at those bands;
RP1 and legacy GPIO retain their own band and clock-profile limits. Experimental
overrides cannot make an Unavailable combination available. Existing conducted
results remain specific to their recorded hardware, clock, mode and RF chain;
they do not establish calibrated power, spectral compliance or all-platform
reliability. [#379](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/379),
[#390](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/390), [#401](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/401)

The new Pi station-draft editor consumes `transmitter-progress/1`; drive progress has
its separate existing control. An advanced firmware draft requiring a newer
view, such as amplifier-aware progress/3, can be refused by the older view.
Use the Pico's corresponding controls; no automatic downgrade or draft
replacement is attempted. Test reference revisions are not firmware image pins
or a claim that every newer firmware/configuration combination is qualified.

Fresh installation/service acceptance of the final Beta artifact set remains
separate from software tests and reused historical evidence. No new hardware,
GPIO, installation, service or RF operation was performed for this preparation.
The earlier FSKCW interruption report did not establish a reproducible
transmitter defect; its issue closure is not an additional continuity fix or
qualification claim. [#427](https://github.com/WsprryPi/WsprryPi/issues/427)
