## Cross-repository traceability rule

Future rig results must identify the exact rig commit, WsprryPi protocol commit or document checksum, fixture checksum, font ID and checksum, application versions, settings, scoring method, and limitations. Product conclusions must cite an immutable rig commit and selected artifact checksums. Uncommitted work must be labelled `UNCOMMITTED`; it must never be cited as though it had a future immutable revision.
